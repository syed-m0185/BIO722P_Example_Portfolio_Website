I am an MSc Bioinformatics student at Queen Mary University of London, with a BSc in Biomedical Science from the University of Surrey. I enjoy applying computational and statistical methods to biological and health data, from cleaning messy and irrational datasets to data interpretation. This is through the usage of Python (Pandas, Numpy, Matplotlib, Seaborn), Jupyter Notebook, SPSS and NCBI BLAST. I carried out a Diabetes Risk Analysis project (https://github.com/skm0518/healthcare-data-analysis-python), where I identified glucose as the strongest correlate of diabetes outcome.  For my undergraduate dissertation, I analysed data from a controlled laboratory to investigate how delayed meal timing and energy restriction affect circadian timing in healthy adult males. I used paired t-tests, Cohen's d and cosinor analysis in SPSS and Microsoft Excel. I found that both central circadian phase (DLMO shift, p = 0.022) and peripheral rhythms (a 7.7 hour delay in plasma glucose acrophase), in conjunction with significantly elevated appetite and food intake.
